effective_date: 2026-09-01

- Standard shipping takes between 3 to 5 business days for delivery.
- Express shipping guarantees delivery within 1 to 2 business days.
- Same-day delivery is available in select metro cities for orders placed before 12:00 PM.
- Standard shipping fee is ₹99 for orders below the free-shipping threshold.
- Orders with a total value of ₹999 or more qualify for free standard shipping.
- Express shipping costs a flat fee of ₹199 regardless of order value.
- Same-day delivery carries an additional fee of ₹299 per order.
- VeriCorp ships to all domestic pin codes across India through verified logistics partners.
- VeriCorp does not currently support international delivery outside India.
- A tracking number is sent via SMS and email within 12 hours of order dispatch.
- Orders placed on Sundays or national holidays are dispatched on the next business day.
