effective_date: 2026-09-01

- Refunds are allowed within 15 days of purchase for all eligible items.
- Opened electronics can be returned within 15 days of delivery.
- Returned products must include all original packaging, manuals, and accessories.
- A restocking fee of ₹250 applies to opened electronics returns.
- Full refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days of receipt at the warehouse.
- Store credit refunds are processed within 24 hours of item inspection.
- Return shipping is free of charge for defective or damaged items.
- Return shipping costs ₹99 for non-defective change-of-mind returns.
- Gift cards, downloadable software, and digital license keys are strictly non-returnable.
- Consumable items such as printer ink and thermal paste are non-returnable once opened.
- Clearance and open-box items marked final sale are not eligible for refunds.
- Proof of purchase or an order invoice number is required for all refund requests.
