- All VeriCorp laptops and desktop computers include a standard 2-year manufacturer warranty.
- VeriCorp headphones, earphones, and audio accessories include a 1-year limited warranty.
- Smart accessories such as cables, chargers, and adapters carry a 6-month limited warranty.
- The standard warranty covers hardware component failures and manufacturing defects.
- Battery degradation exceeding 20 percent capacity loss within the first year is covered under warranty.
- Accidental damage, water exposure, and physical drops are not covered under standard warranty.
- Unauthorized disassembly, third-party repairs, or serial number alteration voids the warranty.
- Cosmetic scratches, dents, and normal wear and tear are strictly excluded from warranty coverage.
- Approved warranty claims receive free doorstep pickup within 3 business days.
- Repaired or replaced items are dispatched within 10 business days of arriving at the service center.
- Replacements provided under warranty carry a 90-day warranty or the remainder of the original warranty, whichever is longer.
