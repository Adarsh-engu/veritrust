- VeriCorp customer support is available Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST.
- Customer support is closed on national holidays and Sundays.
- Live chat support is accessible through the VeriCorp website during active support hours.
- Email support can be reached at support@vericorp.in with 24/7 ticket submission.
- Email inquiries receive a first response within 24 hours of ticket submission.
- Phone support is available at toll-free number 1800-123-8374 during operational hours.
- Average phone hold time is under 5 minutes during standard operational hours.
- Self-service setup guides and video tutorials are available free of charge on the support portal.
- On-site hardware installation is not included with standard product purchases.
- Paid professional home installation is available for televisions and home theater systems at ₹799.
- Remote software diagnostic assistance is provided free of charge for all in-warranty products.
- Priority support response within 2 hours is available exclusively for VeriCorp Care Plus members.
