- VeriCorp accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and RuPay credit and debit cards.
- UPI payments via Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM are accepted for all transactions.
- Net banking is supported across all major Indian scheduled banks.
- Cash on delivery is available for orders with a total value up to ₹10,000.
- Cash on delivery orders incur a convenience handling fee of ₹49.
- No-cost EMI options are available on credit card orders with a minimum value of ₹3,000.
- Standard EMI tenures of 3, 6, 9, and 12 months are offered by partner banks.
- Failed payment debits are automatically reversed by the payment gateway within 5 business days.
- Incomplete payment sessions are cancelled after 15 minutes and reserved stock is released.
- A GST-compliant tax invoice is generated electronically and emailed immediately upon payment confirmation.
- Physical tax invoices are included inside the shipping package for all delivered products.
- Payment details cannot be edited or updated once an order has been placed.
